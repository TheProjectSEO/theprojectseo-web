# TheProjectSEO qualified-demand plan

Objective: create a repeatable path to 5–10 qualified company leads per month for SEO, GEO and connected search services.

Last updated: 2026-07-29

## Define the result

A qualified lead is a company contact with:

- A valid company website and business email
- A problem TheProjectSEO can solve
- A realistic path to the USD 3,500 Initial, USD 5,500 Growth or custom Enterprise scope
- An identified decision maker or implementation owner
- A viable market, timeline and next step

Form fills from students, job seekers, vendors, spam, unsupported budgets or unrelated requests do not count.

## Planning model

This is a planning assumption, not a forecast or guarantee:

| Funnel stage | Monthly planning range |
|---|---:|
| High-intent visits to priority commercial and proof pages | 250–500 |
| Enquiries at a 4% working conversion rate | 10–20 |
| Qualified companies at a 50% acceptance rate | 5–10 |
| Attended discovery calls at a 60% show rate | 3–6 |

The percentages must be replaced with first-party baselines as soon as enough data exists. A large volume of low-intent blog traffic is not a substitute for the 250–500 high-intent visits.

## The four demand loops

### 1. Capture an existing buying decision

Priority questions include choosing an AI-native SEO agency, technical implementation, audits, ecommerce SEO, enterprise SEO, Shopify SEO, AI-search visibility and attribution.

Actions:

1. Use the 50 canonical prompt families as the commercial demand map.
2. Assign every family to one page owner.
3. Improve existing tier-one pages before creating planned platform pages.
4. Make the direct answer, scope, proof, limitations, pricing and next step visible without forcing a reader through the entire page.
5. Build internal links from research, glossary, case studies and methodology into the relevant commercial page.

Primary page cohort:

- `/`
- `/services/seo`
- `/services/seo-audit`
- `/services/technical-seo`
- `/services/content`
- `/services/ecommerce-seo`
- `/services/enterprise-seo`
- `/seo-for-shopify`
- `/services/ai-search`

- `/services/ai-search/llm-visibility-tracking`
- `/services/analytics/seo-attribution`
- `/methodology`
- `/case-studies`
- `/pricing`

### 2. Become a source worth selecting

Generated-answer visibility should come from stronger sources, not manufactured mentions.

Actions:

1. Publish the AI visibility measurement method with official sources and clear limitations.
2. Turn approved project work into evidence records: setup, source, date, observation, limitation and decision.
3. Add comparison criteria buyers can reuse when evaluating an SEO or GEO agency.
4. Keep company, people, pricing, services, projects and locations consistently named.
5. Verify search-discovery and user-fetch crawler access separately from model-training policies.
6. Run the 200-prompt registry as a controlled observation panel and use gaps as a page-improvement backlog.

The 50 commercial prompt families measure late-stage discovery and provider selection. They do not define the editorial calendar. Problem-led resources, diagnostics, datasets, and tools create the earlier discovery path and give independent publishers a reason to cite the work.

Use `docs/problem-led-content-authority-plan.md` as the operating policy for problem selection, linkable assets, Wikipedia, outreach, and authority measurement.

### 3. Convert trust into a qualified conversation

Actions:

1. Keep a contextual CTA after the direct answer, proof, service scope, pricing and final objection sections.
2. Carry the selected USD 3,500, USD 5,500 or Enterprise plan into the lead form and notification.
3. Ask for business email, website, budget and problem context.
4. Show what happens after submission and what information will be reviewed.
5. Keep one short form on commercial pages and the full qualification form on contact.
6. Review mobile CTA visibility, form completion, field errors and confirmation behavior.

### 4. Distribute expertise without disguised promotion

Channels:

- Reddit: contribution and research criticism, no funnel
- LinkedIn: founder-led implementation notes and annotated evidence
- Email: useful follow-up for known contacts who have opted in
- Partner referrals: developers, analytics specialists, ecommerce implementers and agencies without technical SEO capacity
- Search communities and podcasts: original methods, release lessons and documented constraints

Every distributed item should answer one real decision completely. Reformatting the same generic article across channels does not create authority.

## 30-day operating cadence

### Every week

- Review GSC and Bing queries landing on the priority commercial cohort.
- Review AI referrals separately by source and landing page.
- Run the tier-one sentinel prompt panel.
- Improve one high-value page from observed source or evidence gaps.
- Publish one first-hand implementation note or primary-source explanation.
- Contribute to existing Reddit threads without links or company promotion.
- Review every enquiry and label qualified, unqualified, spam or unknown.

### Every month

- Compare high-intent sessions, enquiry rate, qualified rate and booked-call rate.
- Review prompt inclusion, recommendation, citations, cited pages, competitors and accuracy.
- Interview sales or the founder about objections and wording used by qualified companies.
- Refresh pricing, scope, people, project evidence and changing platform claims.
- Decide whether to improve an existing owner, create one missing page or make no page change.

## Measurement dashboard

### Visibility

- Non-brand impressions and clicks for the priority commercial cohort
- Ranking-query coverage by problem, service, platform and industry
- Brand inclusion and recommendation rate by controlled AI condition
- Citation share and cited-URL distribution
- Competitor co-occurrence and factual accuracy

### Engagement

- High-intent landing sessions
- CTA clicks and form starts
- Form completion rate
- Mobile versus desktop completion
- Return visits to pricing, case studies and methodology

### Commercial

- Enquiries by source page and source
- Selected plan and monthly budget
- Qualified lead acceptance rate
- Discovery call booked and attended
- Opportunity, proposal, win and revenue
- Days from first attributable visit to qualified conversation

## Qualification and attribution workflow

1. The site stores the source page, source URL, referrer and UTM values with each lead.
2. Pricing selection is carried into the form and stored with the lead.
3. AI referral sources are classified separately from generic referral traffic.
4. The lead owner assigns qualified, unqualified, spam or unknown within two business days.
5. CRM stages preserve the original source and page-owner cohort.
6. Monthly reporting shows visibility, lead quality and revenue as separate layers.

## Guardrails

- Do not guarantee 5–10 leads or a model recommendation.
- Do not create 200 near-duplicate landing pages for 200 prompt variations.
- Do not use Reddit for covert brand seeding.
- Do not call Ahrefs response counts first-party visits.
- Do not publish a case study without source, date, scope and limitation.
- Do not optimize pageviews at the expense of the qualified-company definition.
