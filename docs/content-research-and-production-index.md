## Important strategic decisions captured in the new backlog

### Exact-match domains

The plan does not say “EMDs work” or “EMDs are dead.” Google describes domain-name words as one of many relevance factors and has an exact-match domain system intended to prevent too much credit. The content program therefore focuses on:

- Brandable versus exact-match decision criteria
- Multi-domain consolidation
- Doorway risk
- Expired-domain due diligence
- Portfolio governance
- Rebrand and migration sequencing

### Estate settlement and executor support

The MeetAlix analog is not a coaching marketplace. It is an estate-settlement service operating across the legal, financial, administrative and personal work an executor faces after a death. The content opportunities therefore focus on:

- What a newly named executor needs to do first
- A stage-based roadmap from first actions through distribution and closure
- Clear ownership across probate, assets, debts, taxes, property and family communication
- Grief-aware task design rather than generic definitions
- Referral due diligence for funeral, hospice, wealth-advisory and real-estate partners
- Jurisdiction, date, source and correction labels on every legal or tax statement

No page should provide individualized legal, tax or fiduciary advice. Every draft needs current jurisdiction-specific primary sources and qualified review.

### Mother-and-baby products and ecommerce

Pigeon Singapore is a mother-and-baby product brand, not an expert marketplace. The content opportunities focus on the product-information problems parents, retailers and support teams face:

- Compatibility across bottles, teats, pumps, sterilizers and replacement parts
- Model, SKU and naming consistency across the brand site, retailers and marketplaces
- Product manuals, FAQs, troubleshooting and support escalation
- Age or feeding-stage guidance with explicit evidence ownership
- Product-to-guide-to-support architecture
- Discussion-first parent research without brand promotion or collection of personal child data

No page should make health, safety, developmental or age-related claims without current product evidence and qualified review.

### India BFSI

The opportunity is not a generic “finance SEO” taxonomy. It is regulated problem solving around:

- APR and Key Fact Statements
- Digital-lending disclosures
- DPDP notices and data journeys
- Rate and calculator freshness
- Insurance Customer Information Sheets
- Grievance pathways
- Financial social-media disclosure
- Multilingual comprehension
- Scam verification
- AI-answer accuracy for financial facts

### Philippines BFSI

The core problems are:

- Verifying lending companies and online lending platforms
- Interest, fee and advance-fee red flags
- Phone-permission and loan-data privacy
- Unauthorized-transaction response
- Complaint routing
- Insurance and HMO assistance
- English and Filipino comprehension
- Official financial-app identity
- AI-answer accuracy for regulated entities

### Recovery housing and addiction treatment

The program is designed around safety and ethical admissions:

- Clear distinctions between levels of care and recovery housing
- Quality and licensing evidence
- Family decision support
- Privacy-safe measurement
- 42 CFR Part 2 considerations
- Advertising certification
- Caller identity and routing integrity
- Insurance-language clarity
- Medication and co-occurring-disorder content

No page should promise treatment suitability, recovery, admission, rankings or compliance.

## Production workflow

1. Select from Priority Queue.
2. Confirm the existing or future commercial owner.
3. Run SERP and community research for the exact problem.
4. Obtain first-party or authoritative evidence.
5. Define the unique asset before drafting.
6. Create a content brief with required reviewers.
7. Draft in TheProjectSEO's practitioner voice.
8. Complete technical, legal, clinical, privacy or native review as required.
9. Publish with a short, problem-led hero and a contextual CTA.
10. Track landing page, problem category, service interest, form completion and qualified-lead disposition.
11. Record the review date, correction path and maintenance trigger.
