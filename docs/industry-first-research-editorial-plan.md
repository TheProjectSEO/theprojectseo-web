# Industry-first research and editorial plan

Last updated: 2026-07-29

## Decision

TheProjectSEO will use SEO communities to test methods and learn from technical criticism. It will use buyer and operator communities to discover problems. Those are different jobs.

The next publishing cycle will not create a large batch of generic service, platform, or industry pages. It will improve existing commercial pages and add a small number of evidence-led resources that solve problems an operator already recognizes.

The complete problem-led content, link-earning, outreach, and Wikipedia policy is documented in:

`docs/problem-led-content-authority-plan.md`

## What a new visitor should find

The site needs four page types:

| Page type | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial page | Explain the service, qualification, scope, proof, pricing, and next step | SaaS SEO, hotel SEO, Shopify SEO |
| Evidence page | Show a real setup, observation, result, limitation, and transferable lesson | Expressway.PH discovery study |
| Operator resource | Solve one expensive industry problem without requiring a sales call | Hotel direct-booking leakage audit |
| Tool or template | Let the reader perform part of the work | GSC-to-CRM cohort worksheet |

The current website already has commercial service and industry coverage. New work should make that coverage more credible and useful instead of duplicating it with near-identical pages.

## Reddit and community research model

### SEO communities

Use SEO communities for:

- Method criticism
- Technical edge cases
- Measurement limitations
- Platform changes
- Practitioner disagreement

Do not treat SEO practitioners as the primary buyer audience.

### Buyer and operator communities

Use industry communities for:

- The exact language people use to describe a problem
- The constraint that prevents a fix
- The workaround people already tried
- The evidence they want before changing a process
- The commercial or operational consequence

Examples already visible in community discussions include:

- SaaS founders describing a structure problem: one generic landing page, few use-case surfaces, and no specific intent ownership
- Shopify merchants debating collection-page value, product-page traffic loss, app weight, weak internal links, and intent mismatch
- Hotel guests abandoning direct booking because the booking engine times out, asks for too much information, or compares poorly with an OTA
- Hotel operators discussing fragmented property technology and vendor support

Community threads are qualitative inputs, not demand-volume estimates. Validate each proposed page with live SERP research, first-party query data, sales language, and business relevance.

## Research-to-publication workflow

1. Collect recurring operator questions, failed fixes, constraints, and requested evidence.
2. Define one reader, one job, one primary page owner, and one measurable business outcome.
3. Research the live SERP and classify the dominant result types.
4. Build a corpus from primary sources, relevant ranking pages, first-party evidence, and direct industry discussions.
5. Create a topic-frequency map and separate table-stakes coverage from information gain.
6. Run the project content optimizer on the research corpus.
7. Remove boilerplate and terms introduced by navigation, unrelated ranking pages, or tool noise.
8. Add first-hand evidence, screenshots, calculations, limitations, and downloadable utility.
9. Edit in the founder-practitioner voice: direct answer first, specific nouns and numbers, no generic setup, no fake certainty, and no tidy AI-sounding conclusion.
10. Publish with descriptive metadata, internal links, accessible visuals, source notes, dates, and the appropriate Article, Dataset, Breadcrumb, or FAQ structured data.
11. Answer relevant community questions natively. Add a link only when current rules allow it and the resource is necessary to complete the answer.
12. Measure qualified entrances, resource use, assisted commercial-page visits, form starts, accepted leads, and sales outcomes separately.

## Backlink evidence standard

Ahrefs domain-level rows do not prove a valuable editorial link. Every named domain must pass a page-level review:

1. The referring page is live and indexable.
2. The reference is in relevant editorial context.
3. The page explains why it cites the target.
4. The target page helps complete the reader's task.
5. The link is not generated by a template, redirect, cache, scrape, profile, mirror, or site-wide component.
6. The page does not exist mainly to link to unrelated domains.
7. The reference persists after the first discovery date.

Initial classification from the 2026-07-29 Ahrefs review:

| Domain | Ahrefs row | Current judgment |
|---|---|---|
| wikipedia.org | DR 97; 1 reported link; 0 followed | Do not claim as a live editorial link until the exact page is verified. The filtered report returned no live backlink. |
| google.com.ph | DR 85; 1 reported link; 1 followed | Unverified. A Google-domain URL can be a product, redirect, cache, or artifact rather than an editorial endorsement. |
| accio.com | DR 75; 3 reported links; 1 followed | Candidate for page-level review. Domain authority alone is insufficient. |
| luke.lol | DR 45; 1 reported link; 1 followed | Likely archive, mirror, or tool behavior; verify before assigning value. |
| myoona.ph | DR 34; 1 reported link; 1 followed | Candidate if the referring page is topically relevant and editorial. |
| uu88.forum | DR 33; 3 reported links; 0 followed | Treat as noise or junk, not evidence of authority. |

Do not disavow every suspicious row automatically. Ignore routine junk unless it creates a security problem, manual-action concern, or another verified risk.

## What not to build

- Eleven platform or industry landing pages without validated demand and differentiated evidence
- Generic “best SEO agency” listicles
- Thin articles that summarize Reddit threads
- A link-building story based only on referring-domain DR
- A case study that equates Ahrefs estimates with first-party visits or revenue
- A claim that Expressway performed because backlinks do not matter
- Promotional Reddit posts disguised as community help
- Wikipedia edits, guest posts, forum profiles, or partner pages created primarily to manufacture links

## Approval gate for each new page

A page enters production only when it has:

- A named audience and operator problem
- A distinct owner in the site architecture
- Evidence that the problem recurs
- A clear information-gain asset
- A first-party or primary-source plan
- A conversion role that does not compromise the answer
- A measurement definition
- Enough material to be genuinely useful without artificial length
